Method: density at 15 °C is solved by iteration from the observed density using the thermal expansion coefficient for the gasoline range (K0 = 346.4228, K1 = 0.4388), the transition range and the jet range, as in API MPMS 11.1 / ASTM D1250. Density at 20 °C is then found from the 15 °C value. This is a simplified implementation without the intermediate rounding steps of the standard, so use the official tables for custody transfer or contract disputes.
